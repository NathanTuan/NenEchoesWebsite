The Questline

Below is a brief overview of the main questline in Nen Echoes.

Video Upload Area

Phase I:Whale Island

You begin your journey on Whale Island, a peaceful village under troubling times. By talking to the NPCs and exploring the environment, you will unlock key mechanics and story elements for the next phase. Upon completing all the quests, you will be ready to embark for the next island.

Video Upload Area

Phase II: At Sea

To capture the boundless scale and thrill of discovery in Nen Echoes, we’ve made sailing the open ocean the core of the exploration experience, seamlessly connecting scattered islands, hidden secrets, and other points of interest across a massive, living world.

Video Upload Area

Phase III: Zaban City

Following the storyline, you find yourself in the bustling metropolis of Zaban City, where the Hunter Exam is rumored to take place. Becoming a Hunter promises glory, fortune, and power. Yet, merely accessing the exam proves difficult. You must talk to local NPCs and finish quests to gather information and unlock the path to the exam. Beware---some NPCS may sell you fake info...
